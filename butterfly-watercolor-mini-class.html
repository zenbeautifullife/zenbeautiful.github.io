What you’ll learn

What We’ll Explore Together

Follow a gentle, step-by-step path from a simple butterfly sketch to a finished painting, while learning how to make the watercolor decisions for yourself.

  • Sketch and plan your butterfly, noticing the shapes and symmetry of its wings.
  • Control water and create soft color transitions.
  • Build transparent layers and add delicate wing details.
  • Make your own choices as you paint, without needing to copy every brushstroke.

Your step-by-step painting path

Inside the mini-course

A peek at the skills you’ll practise in each lesson.

LESSON 1

Plan your butterfly

Start with a simple butterfly outline and observe the shapes and symmetry of the wings.

LESSON 2

Control water and color

See how paper and brush wetness affect your painting, then practise blended colors and soft, transparent washes.

LESSON 3

Paint the wings

Build the main wing shapes and colors with delicate transitions and luminous watercolor effects.

LESSON 4

Add depth and details

Layer after the first washes dry, adding deeper colors, wing patterns and details.

LESSON 5

Complete your butterfly

Balance colors, edges and details, and learn when to stop before overworking your watercolor.

A few helpful details

Questions about the pilot?

Who is this mini-course for?

It’s designed for beginner and developing watercolor learners who want a calm, structured way to paint a butterfly. You don’t need to paint perfectly—bring your watercolor supplies, curiosity and willingness to experiment.

What will the course include?

Step-by-step video lessons, a printable butterfly outline, a color-mixing guide, a painting practice sheet, and personal feedback on one finished butterfly painting.

What materials will I need?

Bring your watercolor supplies. You can use the paints, paper and brushes you already have as you follow the lessons.

When will the course be delivered?

The course is in development. I plan to deliver the lessons and resources digitally within 3–4 weeks of your purchase. Your specific expected delivery date will be communicated before or at purchase, and I’ll provide updates if the schedule changes.

What if the pilot is cancelled or delivery is delayed?

If I cancel the pilot and cannot deliver the promised course, I’ll provide a full refund. If delivery is delayed, I’ll communicate the revised timeline and appropriate options.
